Chapter 1 · The bill that never ends

You are already paying for a solar plant. You just don't own one.

हर महीने का बिल, सालों का किराया।

A family paying ₹3,000 a month hands over ₹9 lakh to the electricity board across 25 years, even if tariffs never rise. All of it is rent. None of it builds anything on your roof.

  • ₹9 lakh

    Paid as rent

    ₹3,000 a month for 25 years at today's tariff. Tariffs usually go up, so the real figure is higher.

  • ₹1.5 lakh

    Paid as EMI instead

    The same family pays roughly ₹2,500 a month for five years, and ends up owning a 3 kW plant.

  • ₹7 lakh+

    Stays with you

    The difference stays in your account. For a society, it stays in the maintenance fund.

Government scheme · PM Suryodaya Yojana

The government pays up to ₹78,000 of your plant. We get it for you.

प्रधानमंत्री सूर्योदय योजना: सरकार दे रही है ₹78,000 तक की सब्सिडी।

In January 2024 the Prime Minister announced the Pradhan Mantri Suryodaya Yojana to put rooftop solar on 1 crore homes. It is now running as PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana, and it is built to give a household up to 300 units of free electricity every month.

The subsidy is paid straight into your bank account after the plant is installed and inspected. In our finance plan that money goes against your loan, which is why the EMI can stay close to your old bill.

The scheme is planned to run through 2026-27. Applying while it is open is the difference between a ₹1.95 lakh plant and a ₹1.17 lakh one.

What we handle for you

  • Registration on the national rooftop solar portal
  • Feasibility approval from your electricity board
  • Net meter application and installation
  • Inspection visit and commissioning report
  • Subsidy claim, tracked until the money is credited

The Sharmas swapped a ₹3,000 bill for a ₹2,486 EMI.

Two ACs, a geyser, a fridge that never rests. Their summer bills had crossed ₹3,000 and nobody wanted to touch savings for a ₹2 lakh plant. They didn't have to. The NBFC paid for a 3 kW system, the ₹78,000 subsidy cut the loan to ₹1.17 lakh, and the monthly outgo actually went down from the first month.

In year six the EMI stops. The roof keeps giving them about ₹2,880 worth of electricity every month.

Chapter 4 · One team, start to finish

You make one phone call. We handle the other six steps.

सर्वे से सर्विस तक, सब कुछ हम संभालेंगे।

No chasing vendors, bank officers or government portals. One team does all of it and stays with you afterwards.

  1. Site recce

    An engineer visits your roof, measures usable area, checks shadows, structure and your meter connection. Free, with no obligation.

  2. Setup suggestion

    We recommend the plant size, panel layout and inverter that fit your roof and your usage, and explain why.

  3. Savings calculation

    A written sheet with your cost, subsidy, EMI and month-by-month savings, so you see the full picture before you sign.

  4. Installation

    Our own crew mounts, wires and tests the plant, arranges the net meter with your electricity board and switches it on.

  5. Subsidy application

    We file the paperwork on the government portal, follow up on inspection and track the claim until the money is credited.

  6. After-setup service

    Monitoring, cleaning guidance, repairs and warranty claims for the life of the plant. This is the step we are known for.
